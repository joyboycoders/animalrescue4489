Our Mission

We Go Where
Others Can't

The Animal Rescue Consortium Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit incorporated in New York in 2024. We specialize in the rescues that no one else will take — street animals in crisis, abuse survivors, and high-risk placements that require dedicated, hands-on intervention.

🚨

Street Rescue

We respond to emergency calls for animals living on the streets of New York and Puerto Rico, providing immediate care and safe placement.

💜

Abuse Recovery

Animals who have suffered abuse need specialized rehabilitation. Our team provides trauma-informed care to help them heal and find loving homes.

🏠

High-Risk Placements

We handle the tough cases — animals with medical needs, behavioral challenges, or those who've been overlooked by other organizations.

🌎

NY, PR & Beyond

From New York City streets to Puerto Rico, we cross borders when animals need us most. No distance is too great when a life is on the line.

About & Contact

Based in New York,
Reaching Beyond

The Animal Rescue Consortium Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization incorporated in New York on October 30, 2024. Founded by Anthony Pagano, we are a fully grassroots, volunteer-driven operation committed to the most vulnerable animals in our communities.

We believe every animal deserves a fighting chance — regardless of how difficult their situation is. From New York City streets to Puerto Rico, we cross every boundary to make that happen.
